HUMAN TYPER - how to open it
============================

1. Make sure you've EXTRACTED this zip first:
   right-click the zip > Extract All... > Extract.
   (Opening the app straight from inside the zip won't work properly.)

2. Double-click "Human Typer.exe".

3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC":
   click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   (It says this because the app isn't registered with Microsoft.)

4. If your antivirus blocks it, allow it.
   Some antivirus programs distrust any app that types for you.

5. In Word, if you see a yellow "Protected View" bar, click "Enable Editing".

How to use it: paste your text, pick a speed, press Start,
then click into your Word document before the countdown ends.
To stop: push the mouse into the top-left corner of the screen, or press Esc.
